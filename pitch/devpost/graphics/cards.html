07 · How it's built

Your wallet starts with your phone number.

Signing in creates a Privy self-custody wallet, and payments move as USDC on Solana devnet. We cover the network fees, so there's no seed phrase or gas to manage.

Accounts
Privy

Sign in with a phone number and an SMS code. It creates the person's own Solana wallet.

Money
USDC on Solana

Digital US dollars that move in about a second. Our backend pays the network fees.

Scam check
Swarms agents

A Watchdog and a Judge review payments to someone new and explain the risk.

Undo
Safe Hold program

A Solana program holds a flagged payment for 24 hours and needs a second OK.

Offline
SMS gateway

Texts reach our server through an httpSMS phone gateway, so sending works without internet.
