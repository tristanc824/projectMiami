What Nest does
• Keeps your money in US dollars (USDC)
• Sends to a phone number in about a second, with no transfer fee
• Lets family receive with no app: open a link, sign in with your number
• Sends and receives by text message when there's no internet
• Checks payments to someone new for scams, with 24 hours to undo a flagged one

Built with: Solana · USDC · Privy · Swarms · React Native · Next.js
