## Title
Nest: A US-Dollar Account for Families Across Borders | SharkByte 2026

## Description
Ana lives in Miami, and her mom lives in Buenos Aires, where prices roughly tripled in a year. Nest is a US-dollar account that keeps their money in dollars.

- Money arrives in about a second, with no transfer fee.
- Her mom needs no app. She opens a link and signs in with her phone number.
- With no internet, you can still send and receive money by text message.
- AI agents check payments to someone new for scams. A flagged payment can be undone for 24 hours.
- Payments need your fingerprint or PIN. You can lock Nest and add a trusted contact.

Built at SharkByte 2026 by Chad Carmichael, Tristan Colombel, Caleb Cruz Torres and Bruno DiBartolo, Florida State University.

Nest runs on Solana devnet with test USDC. Ana and her mom are an illustrative story.
