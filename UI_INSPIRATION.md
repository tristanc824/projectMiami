## The five ideas that matter most for Guard
1. **Hierarchy over decoration** (#3, #4, #5): the balance is loudest, one accent color, no boxes where spacing works.
2. **Whole cards are tappable** (#2): no extra buttons inside cards.
3. **Buttons with depth and real states** (#28, #30): one component, size variants, a top highlight.
4. **Motion with purpose** (#11, #16, #22): an island-style status pill, a logo bounce on launch, rolling numbers on money.
5. **Passwordless sign-in** (#12): his password tips point to the bigger win, no password at all, which is Guard's passkey flow.
