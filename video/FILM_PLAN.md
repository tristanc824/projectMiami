# Nest film plan: motion graphics only

Nov 7, 2026. Built from four research passes covering 100 demo and explainer videos: 25 fintech apps, 25 software launch films, 25 security explainers, and 25 hackathon winners plus cross-border money explainers. None of the researchers could watch video, so their notes come from studio case studies, storyboard frames, descriptions and chapter markers. Rules for craft are in PRODUCTION_BIBLE.md.

**Format:** 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps, about 85 s (90 s maximum). Motion graphics only: the real app UI, type, shapes, a simple map and icons. No filmed or AI-generated people. People appear only inside the app (Mamá's photo, avatars, a message bubble).
**Voice:** Gia (ElevenLabs v4), as Ana, steady and warm. **Martha writes the final lines.** The lines below are drafts for timing.
**Music:** track D (minimal piano and synth pluck, 116 BPM). Cuts land on the beat.

## The big ideas

1. **One motif carries the film: a moss-green dot is Ana's money.** It draws the dollars line (Wealthsimple's single dot), flies Miami to Buenos Aires as an arc, lands in Mamá's balance, comes back on undo, and finally becomes the logo's three rings. One object the eye can follow from start to end. Used by Phantom, Wealthsimple and Zelle.
2. **The camera does every transition.** Match cuts on shared shapes, pushes through one screen into the next. No crossfades. Used by Linear, Superhuman, Framer and Airtable.
3. **Three chapters, one quiet marker each.** "Save", "Send", "Protect" appear as a small label with the Nest mark in the top-left corner, the same style as the website. From Brex, Framer and Notion.
4. **Show the actual number moving,** never a claim. $1,000 shrinking to $321, 0.9 s on a timer, a $0 fee tag. From Wise, Nubank, Plaid and FT.
5. **Scams stay plain; only Nest is amber.** The scam is just a grey message bubble. No hooded figures, no red, no alarm sounds. The film ends on a resolved state, never on a threat. From Apple, Google, Monzo and Starling; the ACCC "fear" spot is the counter-example.
6. **Firsts.** None of the 100 videos showed offline sending, undoing a payment, or a family safe word. We give those room.

## Motion rules (from the software films)

- Ease out fast and settle long. A small overshoot only on checkmarks and toggles.
- Harsh, linear motion only for the "broken" moments: the old bank route, the falling peso.
- Text is revealed from behind a mask. Short keywords only, because the narrator carries the meaning.
- One motion at a time. Every key state (a balance, an arrival, the undo) holds still for at least 1 second.
- Taps are a soft moss ripple. No animated hands, no fake cursor.
- 1 to 2 s per beat, 6 to 12 s per feature.
