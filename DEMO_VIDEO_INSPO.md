# Demo video inspiration for Nest (60-90s, phone UI, emotional story)

Every link below was checked on 2026-11-07. YouTube titles, channels, lengths and dates come from the live video pages. The Vimeo link was checked through Vimeo oEmbed and the X post through its API. All are official brand uploads except #2, which is the director's cut on the production company's Vimeo.

The "What to watch for" notes come from each video's official description, its chapter markers (the timestamps shown) and its runtime. No frames were checked, so watch each one before you borrow a specific beat.

## Start here (most relevant to Nest)
