# Nest: the global money problems it solves

Researched November 7, 2026. Ranked by scale × how directly Nest solves it × how well it demos. **UNVERIFIED** means only secondary sources were found, so check the primary source before it goes on a slide. Positioning reminder: Nest is a neobank and super financial tool. See [NEST_FEATURES.md](./NEST_FEATURES.md).

## The framing sentence
> "Billions of people already carry a phone, but the financial system still charges them, at every step, to save, get paid, spend and protect their own money. Nest puts digital dollars on Solana in every pocket, so those fees and delays disappear."

## Lead the pitch with these three
1. **900 million unbanked adults already own a phone** (the opener: biggest number, most authoritative source).
2. **Freelancers getting paid across borders** (the core live demo: Solana's speed and cost against PayPal and Payoneer).
3. **The US fee trap.** Congress repealed the overdraft-fee cap in May 2025; Nest makes overdrafts impossible by design (timely, close to home for US judges).
4. *If there's time:* merchant QR checkout against card fees (the cheapest extra Solana moment).
