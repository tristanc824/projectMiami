# Where money apps fail people, and what Nest does about it

Research date: Nov 7, 2026. All sources were accessed on that date, and each one shows its own publication date where known. Robinhood is covered in ROBINHOOD_CASE_STUDY.md. **[Unverified]** marks claims backed only by user reports, a single secondary source, or my own reading of the law.

Evidence quality, best first: regulator orders > ombudsman/court decisions > reputable news > review sites and forums. Review sites over-represent bad outcomes. Use them to spot patterns, not to count how often something happens.

---

## 1. Per app: the biggest documented failures

### Cash App (Block)
1. **Fraud disputes not properly investigated.** CFPB order (Jan 16, 2025): investigations were "woefully incomplete". Cash App told fraud victims to ask their bank to reverse the charge and then fought those reversals. Its terms implied that the linked bank handled disputes. Block must pay up to $120M in redress plus a $55M penalty. Sources: [CFPB press release, Jan 16 2025](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-orders-operator-of-cash-app-to-pay-175-million-and-fix-its-failures-on-fraud/), [CFPB enforcement page](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/enforcement/actions/block-inc/).
2. **No human to reach.** For years the phone number printed on the Cash Card and in the terms played only a recording that pointed back to the app. The order requires 24-hour live-person service, with live phone support at least 12 hours a day and live chat at least 18 hours a day. Sources: [CFPB, Jan 16 2025](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-orders-operator-of-cash-app-to-pay-175-million-and-fix-its-failures-on-fraud/), [American Banker, Jan 2025](https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/cfpb-orders-cash-app-to-pay-175-million-for-fraud-failures).
3. **Accounts locked for long periods.** Redress covers "accounts locked for long periods" and denied provisional credit during slow investigations. Source: [CFPB, Jan 16 2025](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-orders-operator-of-cash-app-to-pay-175-million-and-fix-its-failures-on-fraud/).
4. **Fake "support" impersonators.** Scammers posing as Cash App support, found through web searches, collected logins. Block knew and was slow to act. Source: same CFPB release.
5. **Weak compliance controls.** 48 state regulators imposed an $80M BSA/AML penalty (Jan 15, 2025). Source: [CSBS, Jan 2025](https://www.csbs.org/node/521831).
   - Reviews repeat the same themes: closures "for a policy violation" with no specifics, and email-only replies. **[Unverified, self-reported]** [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/cash.app?page=3).

### Revolut
1. **Most fraud complaints of any UK firm.** Which? FOI data (Oct 1, 2025): 3,242 APP-scam complaints went to the Financial Ombudsman in 2024, plus 2,631 other fraud complaints, more than any other firm. In 2025 the ombudsman sided with the customer in 30% (APP) and 37% (other fraud) of cases. Source: [Which?, Oct 1 2025](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/revolut-still-the-worst-uk-firm-for-fraud-complaints-warns-which-axqr43W94wLn).
2. **Thousands of refund requests denied.** Bloomberg reported that Revolut refused thousands of UK reimbursement requests from scam victims (Jul 2024). Source: [Bloomberg Law, Jul 2024](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/banking-law/revolut-gets-more-scam-complaints-than-uk-banks-ombudsman-says).
3. **Chat-only support that loops.** A fraud victim described support as scripted replies and "AI loops" (Irish Times, Jul 7 2025). Revolut says reported fraud triggers a human, and the ombudsman says it cannot force Revolut to offer phone support. Sources: [Irish Times, Jul 7 2025](https://www.irishtimes.com/your-money/2025/07/07/a-revolut-user-encounters-evasive-customer-service-after-1850-fraud), [FOS decision DRN-2679769](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-2679769.pdf).
4. **FX costs that change with the clock.** Standard customers pay an extra 1% on weekend exchanges plus a fair-usage fee above a monthly allowance, and the markup is built into the rate rather than shown as a fee. Source: [Revolut help centre](https://help.revolut.com/en-LT/help/wealth/exchanging-money/how-much-does-it-cost-to-make-an-exchange/will-i-be-charged-for-exchanging-foreign-currencies).
5. **Growth outran controls.** Lithuania fined Revolut €3.5M for AML monitoring gaps (Apr 8, 2025). The full UK banking licence was still held up over risk controls as of Oct 2025. Sources: [LRT, Apr 8 2025](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2531818/revolut-bank-fined-eur3-5m-for-breaching-anti-money-laundering-rules), [Irish Times, Oct 14 2025](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2025/10/14/revoluts-full-uk-banking-licence-held-up-by-concerns-over-global-risk-controls).
   - Sudden freezes are a long-running complaint, and 2025 sanctions-driven freezes hit Russian-speaking EU customers. Source: [Meduza, Nov 3 2025](https://meduza.io/en/feature/2025/11/03/russian-clients-living-in-the-e-u-struggle-to-regain-banking-access-after-fintech-company-revolut-freezes-accounts).

### Wise
1. **Fees, exchange rates and refunds.** CFPB order (Jan 30, 2025): Wise misled US users about ATM fees, failed to disclose exchange rates properly, and did not refund fees when money arrived late. The penalty was cut to about $45K in May 2025, but the roughly $450K redress stayed. Sources: [CFPB, amended order May 2025](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-amends-wise-order-for-remittance-practices/), [Consumer Finance Monitor, Feb 4 2025](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2025/02/04/cfpb-orders-remittance-transfer-provider-to-pay-almost-2-5-million-for-alleged-illegal-activities/).
2. **Account shut instantly, money held.** In one UK ombudsman case (Sept 2024), the account was deactivated "with immediate effect" and a £10,830 balance was withheld pending review. Source: [FOS DRN-5361262](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-5361262.pdf).
3. **Complaints not handled.** Wise paid compensation for failing to acknowledge a complaint about a deactivation. Source: [FOS DRN-4071724](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/decision/DRN-4071724.pdf).
4. **Appeals blocked by the closure itself.** Users say appealing requires logging into the account Wise has already deactivated, and that verification reviews drag on for weeks to months. **[Unverified, self-reported]** [BBB](https://prod.bbb.org/us/ny/new-york/profile/money-transfers/wise-us-inc-0121-166054/complaints), [Sikayetvar, Oct 2025](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/wise-us/wise-holding-my-money-for-months-despite-completed-verification).
5. **Weak AML oversight.** Wise paid $4.2M in a six-state settlement over its AML program (Jul 2025). Source: [InsuranceNewsNet](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/wise-to-pay-4-2m-boost-aml-oversight-in-6-state-settlement).

### Chime
1. **Slow refunds after closing accounts.** CFPB order (May 7, 2024): Chime promised to refund balances within 14 days but in thousands of cases took more than 90. It paid at least $1.3M in redress and a $3.25M penalty. Sources: [CFPB, May 2024](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/newsroom/cfpb-takes-action-against-chime-financial-for-illegally-delaying-consumer-refunds/), [consent order PDF](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_chime-financial-inc-consent-order_2024-05.pdf).
2. **Sudden closures.** The account agreement allows Chime to freeze or close "for any reason with or without notice". ProPublica's review found closed-account issues in 197 of 920 complaints. This is 2021 data and older than the focus window, but it is the root of the pattern. Local news kept reporting the same story into 2023. Sources: [ProPublica, 2021](https://www.propublica.org/article/chime/amp), [WSMV, Apr 7 2023](https://www.wsmv.com/2023/04/07/banking-app-closes-womans-account-freezing-money-needed-pay-bills/?outputType=amp).
3. **Remittance app (Sendwave) misled users.** CFPB (Oct 2023): Sendwave promised money "within seconds" when it often took longer, misstated fees, made users sign liability waivers, and handled errors poorly. Chime paid $3M. Sources: [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-takes-action-against-operator-of-sendwave-app-for-illegally-cheating-people-on-international-money-transfers), [ABA Banking Journal, Nov 1 2023](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2023/11/chime-inc-agrees-to-pay-cfpb-3-million-to-resolve-remittance-app-allegations/).
4. **Disputes denied with "no error found".** A 2025 consumer lawsuit alleges Chime closed unauthorized-transaction disputes without a real investigation. **[Allegation, not a finding]** [court filing](https://consumerattorneys.com/files/recent-lawsuit/file-68fbad8eea27d.pdf).
5. **Fees for faster access to your own pay.** MyPay is free if you wait 1-2 days, or about $2 for instant access. Source: [LendEDU](https://lendedu.com/blog/chime-mypay/). This is a fee model, not a violation.

### Nubank (Brazil, Mexico)
1. **Accounts blocked without warning, salaries held.** On Jun 30, 2025, users reported balances and salaries frozen for up to 72 hours for a "security review" with no notice. Sources: [Olhar Digital, Jun 30 2025](https://olhardigital.com.br/2025/06/30/internet-e-redes-sociais/nubank-bloqueia-salario-clientes-denunciam-falha-no-app/), [Correio, Jun 2025](https://www.correio24horas.com.br/salvador/clientes-reclamam-de-cancelamento-do-nada-de-contas-nubank-tem-mais-de-r-10-mil-preso-0625).
2. **Closures without a reason, and courts pushing back.** The contract lets Nubank close an account with 5 days' notice and no reason. Courts have ordered accounts reopened and awarded damages (for example R$8K in Ceilândia, Mar 2026). Sources: [Jornal Opção](https://www.jornalopcao.com.br/ultimas-noticias/contas-bloqueadas-e-dinheiro-retido-clientes-do-nubank-recorrem-a-justica-apos-suspeita-de-conduta-ilicita-802736/), [O Imparcial, Mar 2026](https://oimparcial.com.br/noticias/2026/03/justica-do-condena-nubank-por-bloqueios-arbitrarios-e-encerramento-de-contas-sem-aviso/).
3. **Pix scam refunds missed or refused.** Courts held Nubank liable where support failed to start Brazil's Pix refund process (MED) quickly enough, or refused it. In May 2026 Brazil's top court (STJ) upheld a R$51K award against Nubank and Inter to an elderly scam victim. Across Brazil in 2023, only about 32% of 2.56M MED refund requests were met. Sources: [Portal Tela, May 26 2026](https://www.portaltela.com/noticias/economia/2026/05/26/nubank-e-inter-sao-condenados-por-falha-que-abriu-golpe-de-r-51-mil-no-pix/), [Contábeis](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/66605/91-dos-pedidos-de-reembolso-de-pix-por-golpe-sao-negados/).
4. **Outages that hide balances.** Users have reported app outages and balances suddenly unavailable. Sources: [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/nubank-esta-fora-do-ar-na-tarde-de-hoje/), [Olhar Digital, Oct 2 2024](https://olhardigital.com.br/2024/10/02/internet-e-redes-sociais/nubank-caiu-ai-app-do-banco-apresenta-instabilidade-nesta-quarta-feira-2/).
5. **Unexpected closures in Mexico (2026).** **[Unverified, single secondary source]** [Descubre, Apr 17 2026](https://www.descubre.vc/noticia/nu-m-xico-bajo-la-lupa-por-reportes-de-cierres-masivos-de-cuentas-2026-04-17).
   - Counterpoint: Nubank has one of the lowest complaint rates per customer in Brazil's central-bank ranking (Q1 2025: 10.6 complaints per million customers). Its failures are about how it handles freezes and scams, not about volume. Source: [Economic News Brasil, May 8 2025](https://economicnewsbrasil.com.br/2025/05/08/ranking-de-reclamacoes-do-banco-central-traz-bancos-mais-reclamados-do-ano/).

### Monzo
1. **Most scam refusals overturned.** Of 3,372 fraud and scam complaints closed in 2024, the ombudsman sided with the customer in 34%, which means more than 1,000 wrongly rejected. That is the highest share among the big banks. In 2025, Monzo's APP-scam uphold rate was 41%, the highest of the top 10 firms. Sources: [The Paypers, Feb 11 2026](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/monzo-wrongly-rejected-refund-complaints-of-fraud-and-scam-victims), [Which?, Oct 1 2025](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/revolut-still-the-worst-uk-firm-for-fraud-complaints-warns-which-axqr43W94wLn).
2. **Fined over financial crime controls.** The FCA fined Monzo £21.1M (Jul 8, 2025). Monzo onboarded customers on implausible information (London landmarks given as home addresses) and signed up more than 34,000 high-risk customers despite a restriction. Source: [FCA, Jul 2025](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-fines-monzo-21m-failings-financial-crime-controls).
3. **Freezes with no explanation.** BBC Watchdog (Oct 2019) found accounts frozen with no reason and weeks without access, and lockdown-era reports continued the story. This is older than the focus window but is the origin of the pattern. Monzo's answer was that the law sometimes bars it from giving a reason. Sources: [Crowdfund Insider, Oct 2019](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2019/10/153035-following-critical-bbc-report-regarding-frozen-accounts-digital-bank-monzo-responds/), [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/bankingtech/monzo-bank-accused-of-freezing-accounts-for-no-apparent-reason-).
4. **Frozen customers lose support too.** Customers with frozen accounts can be barred from the phone line. **[Unverified, single report]** [Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/monzo-faces-a-chorus-of-customer-complaints-over-frozen-accounts-and-stolen-money/).

### Not one of the six apps, but directly relevant to Nest
- **Synapse (2024):** when this banking middleman collapsed, about 100,000 users of apps like Yotta and Juno lost access to roughly $265M. Records did not match, leaving a $60-95M shortfall. In Nov 2025 the CFPB paid out $46M, about half the gap. Lesson: users blame the app, not the partner. Sources: [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/cfpb-hold-synapse-accountable-missing-customer-funds-90-million-mcwilliams/757330/), [Crowdfund Insider, Dec 2025](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2025/12/256754-cfpb-allocates-46m-to-victims-of-synapse-fintech-collapse/).
- **Circle can freeze USDC.** Circle has blacklisted roughly 370-500 addresses holding about $110M since launch, including a $57M freeze in May 2025 tied to a court case. Sources: [AMLBot](https://blog.amlbot.com/circle-froze-16-business-hot-wallets-including-a-blockchain-bridge-smart-contract/), [Spark research](https://www.spark.money/research/stablecoin-blacklisting-sanctions-mechanics). Exact counts are **[Unverified]**.
- **FDIC claims are policed.** The FDIC has sent cease-and-desist letters to crypto and fintech firms that implied deposit insurance (2022-2023). Source: [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/fdic-continues-to-police-crypto-industry-deposit-insurance-claims).

## 2. Patterns across apps, ranked by how much they hurt trust

| # | Pattern | Seen at | Nest exposure today |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Money frozen or account closed with no reason, timeline or exit.** "For any reason, with or without notice." | Cash App, Chime, Wise, Nubank, Monzo, Revolut | High. Circle can freeze USDC, and Nest will need its own compliance holds. No screen covers this. |
| 2 | **No human to reach**, or chat bots that loop, often worst exactly when the account is frozen | Cash App (CFPB-ordered fix), Revolut, Wise, Nubank, Monzo | High. No help screen exists. |
| 3 | **Scam and fraud losses not refunded; disputes closed without real investigation** | Cash App, Monzo, Revolut, Nubank, Chime | Partly covered by Undo (new recipients only, 24h). Nothing for any other case. |
| 4 | **Slow money back** after a closure or a failed payment | Chime (14 days promised, 90+ taken), Wise, Nubank | Medium. No withdraw-on-closure path. |
| 5 | **"Where is my money, really?"** Partner or custody failure, unclear protection | Synapse/Yotta/Juno, Revolut's UK licence limbo | High. Nest is not a bank, and the user must hear that plainly once. |
| 6 | **Hidden or shifting FX and fees; overpromised speed** | Wise (CFPB), Revolut weekend markup, Sendwave "within seconds" | Medium. "$0 fee" and "Arrives in ~1s" are strong claims. The peso rate is an example, and cash-out fees are not shown. |
| 7 | **Fine print that shifts responsibility** (terms say "ask your bank", liability waivers) | Cash App, Sendwave, Chime | Low today. Keep it low: never point users elsewhere for disputes. |
| 8 | **Lost access after a lost phone or number change** | Cash App (recovery codes go to the old number), impersonator "support" lines | High. Passkeys tie the account to devices, and no recovery flow exists. |

Note on alarming notifications: I found no regulator finding on this. The closest case is Nubank's Jun 2025 72-hour holds with no notice, where silence did the damage, not alarm. Treat it as a design principle, not a documented pattern. **[Unverified as a pattern]**

## 4. What Nest should change: the 10 highest-impact, simplest changes

Ranked by trust impact per unit of effort. Each one maps to an existing screen or one new screen. Copy is a draft written to the brand rules.

1. **Lost-phone recovery.** On Welcome (1), add a small "Lost your phone?" link under "I already have one". It opens one screen: "Sign in with your other device" (synced passkey), or "Ask Mamá to confirm it's you" (trusted contact) + ID check. On Face ID (2), add one line: "Saved to your iCloud Keychain." Fixes pattern 8. Passkeys with no recovery path are the fastest way to lock people out of their own money.

2. **New screen: "Account on hold."** Never freeze silently. It shows a plain reason category ("We need to check a payment" / "We need your ID" / "Circle has paused these dollars"), the one thing the user can do, the date we'll answer by, "Your money is still yours", and "Talk to a person". If we close the account, it shows the date the money will be sent out. Fixes patterns 1 and 4, which were the biggest trust killer at every app studied.

3. **A human, with a reply time.** Add a "Help" row in You: "Talk to a person · replies in about 5 min". Link it from the on-hold screen and the sign-in screen. Never route people to a phone number that only plays a recording (the Cash App lesson). Fixes pattern 2.

4. **"Something wrong?" on every payment.** Put it on the transaction detail and on the Arrived receipt (11): "Report a problem", then the user picks from three options (I didn't make this / Scam / Didn't arrive). Give each report a status line in Activity ("Reviewing · answer by Nov 12"). Undo stays the fast path, and this covers every other case. Fixes pattern 3. **[Unverified]** Whether US dispute rules (Reg E) apply to a USDC wallet is a legal question, so treat it as if they do.

5. **"Lock Nest" switch.** Make it the first row on Security (14): one tap stops all sends until Face ID unlocks it. Also offer it from the new-sign-in notification ("Not you? Lock Nest."). It works like freezing a card, before Nest has a card.

6. **Limits shown only when they matter.** On Amount (9), add a third chip when close to a limit: "Up to $2,000 today". Add a Limits row in You. No surprise "payment failed" at Confirm. Fixes the "unclear limits" complaint.

7. **Rate and fee you can check.** On Amount (9) and Confirm (10), tapping "≈ ARS 242,500" shows "1 USD = 970 ARS · source · 9:41 PM". Keep the "Example" label until a live feed exists (Pyth has no ARS feed). Show the cash-out fee before confirming, even when it is $0. Keep "Arrives in ~1s" truthful: if a send is queued or held, the chip changes. Fixes pattern 6, the Wise and Sendwave lessons.

8. **One honest line about what Nest is.** On Home (4), tapping "Held in US dollars" opens one sheet: "Digital dollars (USDC, issued by Circle). Not a bank account. Not FDIC insured." Add the same line as a footer on Welcome (1). Never imply insurance. Fixes pattern 5 and heads off an FDIC misrepresentation problem.

9. **Calm notifications for every money event.** Write each one as a single line stating a fact, never an alarm: "Mamá has your $250." / "$120 from Daniel." / "Payment on hold. We'll answer by Tue." / "New sign-in on iPad. Not you? Lock Nest." Make them a spec sheet, not a new screen. Silence during a freeze was the Nubank failure.

10. **Get paid like a bank account.** On Receive (5), add a row under "Share my link": "Get paid by direct deposit" → account and routing number. Add "Statements" and a search field to Activity's "See all". This turns Nest from a wallet into an account someone can get a salary into. **Needs partner** (virtual-account provider). Statements and search do not.

What Nest already gets right, so keep it: Undo for new recipients and the scam check (pattern 3, the Monzo/Revolut lesson), $0 fees shown before sending, and receipts with a timestamp. The Confirm screen (10) already says up front whether a payment can be undone. That is the honesty that Cash App's terms lacked.
